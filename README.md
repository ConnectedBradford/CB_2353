# CB_2353_Exploring the wider health determinants associated with Looked After Children

Bradford Children’s Trust in partnership with the Bradford Local authority have approached the team to request help in analysing the data associated with Looked After Children (LAC).

The project will take a holistic analytical approach to develop understanding of LAC and service provision across the Bradford region. 
We will begin by conducting a comprehensive analysis of the nature, scale, and distribution of LAC across the region as recorded in relevant datasets. Subsequently, working closely with the NIHR Health Determinants Research Collaboration and the Children’s Trust we will explore how LAC are represented across other public service data housed within the Connected Bradford platform. These data sets are likely to include observations at the pseudonymised individual level – i.e. characteristics of young people from their associated health (primary care interactions, CAMHS, A&E admissions) and education records (school readiness, absences, etc), and observations which account for area level factors such as indices of deprivation (recorded by the census) and level of West Yorkshire Police crime/incidents within the home LSOAs of young people.
